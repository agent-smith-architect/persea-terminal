# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project
spaces agrees to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the
work. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers at
persea.org@proton.me. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
The maintainers apply the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.

Report security vulnerabilities through the [security policy](SECURITY.md), not
through this channel.
